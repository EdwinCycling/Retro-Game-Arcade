# 🎮 Universal Controls & Input Guide

The **Classic Retro Arcade** supports four distinct control schemes out of the box: Keyboard, Gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation), Mobile Tilt (Gyroscope), and Touch Screen Gestures.

---

## 1. Keyboard Controls

### Universal Navigation & System Keys
- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Steer, move, jump, duck
- **Spacebar**: Primary Action (Fire, Jump, Swing Crowbar, Hit Ball)
- **P**: Pause / Unpause Game
- **M**: Mute / Unmute Chiptune Audio
- **R**: Restart current session
- **ESC / Back Button**: Exit cabinet and return to Arcade Lobby

### Specific Game Bindings

- **Up Arrow / W**: Accelerate (Up to 293 km/h in High Gear)
- **Down Arrow / S**: Brakes

## 3. Mobile Tilt (Gyroscope Motion Controls)

Supported on modern iOS (Safari) and Android (Chrome) devices equipped with an accelerometer and gyroscope.

### How to Use:
1. Tap the **Tilt & Touch** button in the lobby or inside the cabinet.
2. In the game control strip, tap **Kantelen / Tilt AAN**.
3. **Calibrate Neutral Position**: Hold your phone in your hands at your comfortable natural playing angle and tap **Kalibreer (Calibrate)**. This subtracts current sensor tilt and treats it as center $(0,0)$.
4. **Steering**:
   - **Tilt phone left/right**: Moves your spaceship, Pac-Man, or car left/right.
   - **Tilt phone forward/backward**: Moves your character up/down.

### iOS Safari Permissions:
When prompted by Safari, tap **Allow** for *Motion and Orientation Access*. If you previously dismissed this prompt:
- Open iOS **Settings** → **Safari** → Enable **Motion & Direction Access**.

## 5. Sudoku & Brain / Logic Controls (1984)

The Sudoku cabinet reproduces an authentic mahogany reading table with newsprint paper and tactile pencil markings.

### Multi-Input Controls:
- **Mouse / Touch**:
  - Tap any cell on the 9×9 grid to select.
  - Tap the on-screen 1–9 number pad to enter a digit into the selected cell.
  - Tap **Timer (Clock)** in the newsprint masthead to toggle the visible running timer (`MM:SS` vs `••:••`).
  - Tap **Potlood (Notes)** mode toggle (or press `N` / Gamepad `Y`) to switch between placing real answers and candidate notes.
  - Tap **Gum (Eraser)** or press `Backspace` / `Delete` to clear the selected cell or note.
  - Tap **Hint (💡)** or press `H` for smart deduction assistance.
  - Tap **Ongedaan Maken (Undo)** or press `U` / `Ctrl+Z` to revert your last move.
  - Tap **Top Scores (Trophy)** in the header to view the local Hall of Fame.

### Scoring & Penalty System:
- **Base Score**: Easy = 5,000 pts | Medium = 7,500 pts | Hard = 10,000 pts | Expert = 12,500 pts
- **Speed Bonus**: +3,000 max points (decays dynamically based on elapsed puzzle solving time).
- **Penalties**:
  - **Hint Deduction**: -800 points per hint used.
  - **Mistake Deduction**: -400 points per invalid digit placed.
  - **Correction Deduction**: -100 points per cell cleared or overwritten after being previously filled.
- **Persistence**: Saved locally in browser `localStorage` across sessions, with initials entry and top-rank celebration.

### Keyboard Shortcuts:
- **Grid Navigation**: `Arrow Keys` or `W / A / S / D`
- **Enter Digits**: `1` through `9`
- **Clear Cell**: `Backspace`, `Delete`, or `0`
- **Toggle Notes Mode**: `N`
- **Undo Move**: `U` or `Ctrl + Z`
- **Request Hint**: `H`
- **Pause / Settings**: `P` or `Escape`

### Gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation Controller):

## 12. Klaverjassen (1890 / 1985 - Amsterdams & Rotterdams)

Klaverjassen is the authentic Dutch 4-player partnership trick-taking game played at a cozy café table with your AI teammate Henk against Ingrid & Jan.

### Multi-Input Controls:
- **Biedfase (Bidding)**:
  - When it is your turn to bid, choose **Spelen (Troef Kiezen)** and select your trump suit (♣, ♦, ♥, ♠), or choose **Passen** to let the next player bid.
- **Speelfase (Tricks)**:
  - Click any card in your 8-card hand that obeys the active system rules:
    - **Amsterdams**: You do NOT have to undertrump if your teammate currently holds the highest card of the trick.

    - **Troefkaarten Waarde**: Nel (9) = 14 pt, Boer (J) = 20 pt, Aas = 11 pt, 10 = 10 pt.
    - **Roem**: Automatically detected and awarded to the winner of the trick (Driekaart 20 pt, Vierkaart 50 pt, Stuk 20 pt).
- **Match Controls**:
  - Toggle between **Amsterdams** and **Rotterdams** rules in the top bar.
  - Switch between a quick match (4 games) or a full **Boompje** (16 games).
  - Inspect the live score sheet (Wij vs Zij, Roem, Nat-boetes).

## 13. Blackjack / 21 (1962 / Vegas Strip Casino Classic)

Blackjack brings the authentic Las Vegas green felt casino experience with chips, doubling, splitting, insurance, and realistic card shoe dealing.

### Multi-Input Controls:
- **Inzetfase (Betting Phase)**:
  - **Fiches**: Click €5 (Red), €25 (Green), €100 (Black), €500 (Purple), or €1000 (Gold) chips to add to your current bet.
  - **Keyboard 1–5**: Quickly place chips 1 to 5 with keyboard number keys.
  - **Wis Inzet (C)**: Clear your current bet back to €0.
  - **2× Inzet (D)**: Double your current bet before the cards are dealt.
  - **DELEN (Space / Enter)**: Confirm bet and deal initial two cards to player and dealer.
- **Speelfase (Player Turn)**:
  - **🟢 Kaart / Hit (H / Space)**: Request another card from the shoe to increase your hand total.
  - **🛑 Passen / Stand (S / Enter)**: Lock in your current score and pass the turn to the dealer.
  - **⚡ Verdubbelen / Double Down (D)**: Double your wager, receive exactly one more card, and immediately stand.
  - **✂️ Splitsen / Split (P)**: When dealt a pair of matching cards, split into two separate hands with equal wagers.
  - **Verzekering (Insurance)**: Offered when dealer shows an Ace (costs 50% of original bet; pays 2:1 if dealer has Blackjack).
- **Ronde Afgelopen (Round Over)**:
  - **Nieuwe Ronde (Space / Enter)**: Settle payouts and start next betting phase.
  - **Herlaad Bankroll**: If bankroll runs empty, reload €1,000 in free casino play chips at any time.
  - **Hall of Fame**: Compete for top lifetime bankrolls and save your 3-letter initials.

## 14. Contract Bridge (1925 / Club - De Koning der Denksporten)

Contract Bridge provides an authentic felt table experience with official bidding box auctions, 5-card major conventions, dummy hand play, and duplicate bridge scoring.

### Multi-Input Controls:
- **Biedfase (Bidding Auction)**:
  - **Niveau Kiezen (1–7)**: Select the contract level 1 through 7 across the top of the bidding box.

  - **REDOUBLET (XX)**: Redouble if opponents have doubled your partnership's bid.
- **Speelfase (Card Play)**:
  - **Uitkomst & Bijspelen**: Click any legal card in your hand to lead or follow suit.
  - **Dummy Besturen**: If South (you) is Declarer, you also select and play cards from North's exposed dummy hand when it is North's turn!
  - **Regels voor Bijspelen**: You must always follow the lead suit if you hold cards in that suit. If void, you may play any card (trump or discard).
- **Hulpmiddelen & Meesterstand**:
  - **Honneurpunten Hulp (Oog-icoon)**: Toggles the live display of High Card Points (A=4, K=3, Q=2, J=1) and suit distribution lengths.
  - **Dossier & Meestergids (❓)**: In-depth guides covering 5-card majors, 1NT openings, the Snit (finesse), and historic tributes to master bridge players.
  - **Meesterstand (🏆)**: Hall of Fame leaderboard tracking high scores and masterpoints.

## 17. Kamertje Verhuren (1895 / Ruitjespapier Klassieker)

Kamertje Verhuren (Dots & Boxes) replicates the nostalgic feeling of sketching lines with a ballpoint pen on 5mm math graph paper during school breaks.

### Multi-Input Controls:
- **Lijntje Tekenen (Balpen Besturing)**:
  - **Muis / Touchscreen**: Beweeg over een open lijnsegment tussen twee stippen om een potlood-schets te zien.
  - **Klik / Tik**: Trek direct een stevige balpen-streep in jouw inktkleur (Blauw voor Speler 1, Rood voor Speler 2/AI).
- **Kamer Voltooien & Kettingreacties**:
  - **Vierde Lijn**: Het voltooien van het vierde lijntje verhuurt het kamertje direct, plaatst een handgeschreven initiaal en levert een extra beurt op!
  - **Ketting Combos**: Speel meerdere kamertjes achter elkaar leeg voor oplopende kettingreactie-chimes.
- **Opties & Wiskundige Functies**:
  - **Rastergrootte (3×3 t/m 6×6)**: Schakel tussen snelduels of grote strategische collegeblok-vellen.
  - **Spelmodi**: Speel solo tegen de computer of met z'n tweeën in 2 Spelers Pass & Play.
  - **Niveaus**: Kies uit Klasgenoot (Novice), Wiskundeleraar (Tactician), en Édouard Lucas (Master met Dubbele-Weggeefzet).
  - **💡 Tip**: Vraag een wiskundige zet-analyse op om te zien welk lijntje veilig is.
  - **Nieuw Blad & Zet Terug**: Scheur een nieuw velletje ruitjespapier af of neem een zet terug.
